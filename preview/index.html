Auth pack — screen flow wireframe (styling comes from the app's design system)

The four screens the cognito-auth skill builds into a delivered app. Self-sign-up is on by default; the done-criterion is that a first-time visitor can reach sign-up from the login page, create an account, and — once it is confirmed — sign in and reach a protected route. Email verification is required in every environment (secure by default), so the confirm-code and resend screens are on the happy path and the delivered app carries no auto-confirm bypass.
